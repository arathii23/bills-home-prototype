Events we would log

Concept prototype for the Paytm Product Campus Case 2 (Bills Home). It follows the look of Paytm's public app screens but is not an official Paytm product, and uses no Paytm assets. Every name, amount and date is made up; nothing here connects to a real account, biller or payment.

About this prototype

A clickable concept of the Bills Home proposal in the Case 2 deck: Find a bill by scanning it, Trust the amount before paying, and Repeat without having to remember.

What is simulated

  • All names, amounts, dates and bill details are made up.
  • No network calls, no real payment, no PIN: the Authorise step is a demo.
  • The AI bill reading is shown as a scripted result.

Design rules shown

  • The photo is deleted after reading; only four fields are kept.
  • No new fee added by Paytm on bills in V1.
  • Bill Day and Autopay are V1.5 previews, offered only after trust is earned: one day a month to pay everything due, and capped Autopay after the 2nd on-time payment.

Look and feel

Based on Paytm's public app screenshots. It is a recreation, not the real app, and uses a text wordmark instead of any Paytm logo.
